About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is a pioneering institution dedicated to training the next generation of professionals in digital and cyber risk management. We bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical application, ensuring our graduates are ready to tackle real-world challenges.

By combining university-level research with industry best practices, IDR creates a unique learning environment where students gain both deep theoretical understanding and hands-on experience. Our programs are designed in collaboration with leading organizations to ensure relevance and immediate applicability in the workplace.
